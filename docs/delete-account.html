Delete Your Account

Last updated: August 10, 2026

We're sorry to see you go. You can delete your Daily Stitch account and all associated data at any time.

Note: Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. All your tasks, habits, streaks, and history will be permanently removed.

Option 1: Delete From Within the App (Fastest)

  1. Open Daily Stitch on your device
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Tap Account Deletion
  4. Confirm your deletion

Your account and all data will be immediately deleted from our servers.

Option 2: Delete Via Email

If you no longer have access to the app, send us an email and we'll process your deletion request within 48 hours.

To: dailystitch79315@gmail.com

Subject: Account Deletion Request

Body: Include the email address associated with your account

What Gets Deleted

  • Your account credentials (email, password hash)
  • All tasks and to-dos
  • All habits and habit data
  • All completion history and streak data
  • All project memberships and daily logs
  • Subscription status (subscription itself is managed by Google Play)

Data Retention

Once your account is deleted, your data is permanently removed from our active databases. Crash reports in Sentry are anonymized and contain no personal data.
