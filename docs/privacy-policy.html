Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Daily Stitch ("we", "our", "app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our mobile application.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only the data necessary to provide the app's core functionality:

  • Email address — used for authentication and account creation
  • Task and habit text — your daily to-dos and habit names, entered by you
  • Completion timestamps — when you mark tasks or habits as done
  • Timezone — your device timezone to manage daily resets correctly
  • Subscription status — managed through Google Play Billing; we never see your payment card details

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide task tracking, habit tracking, and streak features
  • To sync your data across devices via your account
  • To manage your subscription status

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties. Your data stays private to you and our app infrastructure.

4. Data Storage & Security

  • All data is stored securely on Supabase (hosted in AWS, region: US East / EU Central)
  • Data is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS
  • Row-Level Security (RLS) ensures no user can access another user's data
  • We use Sentry for crash reporting with PII (personally identifiable information) scrubbing enabled

5. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — backend infrastructure and authentication
  • Google Play Billing — subscription and payment processing
  • Sentry — crash reporting (anonymized)

We do not use Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, or any other analytics/tracking SDK.

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, all your data (tasks, habits, logs, projects) is permanently removed from our servers.

7. Your Rights

  • Access your data — you can view all your data within the app
  • Export your data — request a full export of your data from the app settings
  • Delete your account — go to Settings → Account Deletion to permanently remove all your data

8. Children's Privacy

The app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
