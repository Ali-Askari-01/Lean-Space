Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Daily Stitch ("we", "us", or "our") is a habit and task tracking application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: Email address (provided during sign-up or via Google Sign-In).
  • Task and habit data: Tasks, habits, completion timestamps, streak counts, and notes you create in the app.
  • Subscription status: Whether you have an active Pro subscription (verified via Google Play purchase tokens).

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the app's core functionality (task tracking, streaks, reminders).
  • To authenticate your account and sync data across devices.
  • To verify your subscription status for Pro features.
  • To respond to support requests when you contact us.

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on our Supabase backend (hosted on Amazon Web Services). All data is transmitted over HTTPS. We do not store your payment details — subscription purchases are processed entirely by Google Play.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

We may share data only in the following circumstances:

  • With service providers who assist in operating our app (e.g., Supabase for backend hosting), under strict data protection obligations.
  • When required by law or to protect our legal rights.

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all associated data (tasks, habits, streaks, subscription records) is permanently deleted.

Your Rights

  • Access & Export: You can request a full export of your data from Settings → Help & Support.
  • Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated data from Settings → Delete Account.
  • Correction: You can edit or delete any task or habit directly in the app.

Children's Privacy

Our app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
