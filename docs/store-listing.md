# Google Play Store Listing — Daily Stitch

## App Name
Habits & To-Do: Daily Stitch

## Short Description (74 chars)
```
Daily to-do list with 5 task limit, habit tracking, streaks & reminders.
```

## Full Description
```
A simple daily planner that helps you stay consistent.

5 TASKS PER DAY
You get exactly 5 tasks each day. No long lists, no clutter.
Just pick what matters and get it done.

HABIT TRACKER
Track up to 5 daily habits. Tap to mark done.
Build streaks by showing up every day.

MISSED TASKS
If you don't finish a task, it shows up the next day.
You decide whether to redo it or let it go. No pressure.

STREAKS & REWARDS
Complete all tasks to keep your streak going.
Earn badges as you hit milestones. Share your progress with friends.

REMINDERS
Set a reminder for each task. Get an evening nudge
if tasks are still open. Never forget what you planned.

PROGRESS VIEW
See your completion rate, current streak, best streak,
and how many perfect days you've had.

7 THEMES
Pick a look you like. Light, dark, or colorful — your choice.

15 LANGUAGES
Works in English, Spanish, French, German, Italian,
Portuguese, Arabic, Hindi, and more.

PRO FEATURES
- Streak Freeze: protect your streak once per month
- Full history of missed tasks
- All 5 habit slots (3 on free)
- Team check-in spaces
- Premium themes

Simple tools. Real consistency. Start your streak today.
```
